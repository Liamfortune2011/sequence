How to play
  • Pick a card from your hand, then click one of the highlighted spaces on the board to place your chip.
  • Get 5 chips in a row (across, down or diagonal) to make a sequence. The gold corners are wild for everyone.
  • J♦ / J♣ (two-eyed jacks) are wild: put a chip on any open space.
  • J♠ / J♥ (one-eyed jacks) remove an opponent's chip (not one that's part of a sequence).
  • Dead card: if both spaces for a card are taken, you can swap it for a new card once per turn, then still play.
  • Two sequences can share one chip. Win by reaching the number of sequences chosen above.
